# NDCR Public Content Audit

**Status:** Evidence-limited audit; no website or CMS implementation  
**Audit date:** 22 October 2026 (Asia/Kolkata)  
**Purpose:** Test the proposed content architecture and schema against accessible public evidence. This is not a complete catalogue export.

## Evidence rules and access limits

Each statement is tagged with one of these evidence classes:

1. **[PUBLIC—DIRECT]** Visible in an official NDCR public platform page or its indexed official track page.
2. **[LOCAL—REFERENCE]** Visible in a file supplied in `docs/references/`; this proves the reference contains the information/artifact, not that the item was published or remains current.
3. **[INFERENCE]** A cautious interpretation of repeated evidence. It is not a verified fact.
4. **[UNKNOWN / INACCESSIBLE]** Not verifiable from the available material or the platform fetch.

The official [SoundCloud profile](https://soundcloud.com/new-delhi-community-radio) could be opened as a JavaScript-disabled snapshot. It identifies the profile as “New Delhi Community Radio,” calls it “NDCR,” locates it in New Delhi, and exposes nine recent track rows. Its page states that JavaScript is disabled; the snapshot was crawled nine months before this audit and is incomplete/stale relative to the audit date. Individual indexed SoundCloud track pages provide some descriptions, dates, genres, artwork indicators, and artist links. The profile’s visible rows are evidence of those rows, not a complete history.

Direct fetches of the official [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@NewDelhiCommunityRadio) and [Instagram account](https://www.instagram.com/newdelhicommunityradio/) failed in the available public-page access tool. Search results can surface isolated indexed items, but they are not a substitute for a verified channel/feed review. No current official-platform cross-check was possible for the individual local-reference items.

**Source hierarchy:** Direct official platform evidence takes priority for published releases and metadata. Local references support artwork/schedule/deck claims only. Secondary search snippets and third-party articles are not used to fill missing set records. Repetition is described as an observed pattern, never promoted to a rule without owner confirmation.

### 1.3 Archive patterns tested against available evidence

- **Numbering:** [PUBLIC—DIRECT] At least the SoundCloud entries 009, 018, 019, 030, 036–040, 043, and 044 display a zero-padded sequence. [LOCAL—REFERENCE] Another artwork says `#070`. This supports use of a numbered series, but does not establish a complete, gap-free sequence, a single numbering source, or whether every upload receives a number. The unnumbered `HouseCats` and `Works Of Intent` rows complicate a rule that every account upload is a numbered set.
- **Weekly Thursday / 18:00 IST:** [LOCAL—REFERENCE] Three dated schedule examples show 18:00 IST on listed dates; the September and October references show Thursday dates. [PUBLIC—DIRECT] SoundCloud publication timestamps span different local times and weekdays in the accessible set sample: e.g. 009 at 17:43 Tuesday; 018 at 00:36 Wednesday; 019 at 00:11 Wednesday; 030 at 17:43 Sunday; 036 at 00:14 Monday; 037 at 01:33 Saturday; 038 at 00:30 Thursday; 039 at 23:43 Sunday; 040 at 00:00 Wednesday; 043 at 00:13 Wednesday; 044 at 11:56 Monday (IST conversions). This suggests platform upload timestamps are not consistently Thursday 18:00. They may differ from scheduled/public announcement times or reflect a release workflow, so the actual public release-time rule remains unverified. Do not derive actual release time from the SoundCloud publication timestamp without NDCR confirmation.
- **Audio/video alternation:** [PUBLIC—DIRECT] SoundCloud gives an audio-platform listing, but that alone cannot confirm the same session’s video counterpart or sequence. [LOCAL—REFERENCE] Platform artwork includes a wide YouTube-labelled ADAMIX #070 and separate SoundCloud square artwork; this demonstrates platform-specific design formats, not historical audio/video alternation. The alternating format claim is not established by the audited records.
- **Artist naming:** [PUBLIC—DIRECT] The series uses inconsistent punctuation/casing: `#009: Aayna`, `#018 : Hamza Rahimtula`, `#019 : KHXDEEJ`, `#030: Su Real`, and profile entries with number/name. Names may be title-cased or all-caps. A canonical artist display name must be maintained separately from verbatim platform title.
- **Genres:** [PUBLIC—DIRECT] Indexed SoundCloud pages expose a single genre field for examples (`Electronic`, `House`, `Techno`). Genres are not uniformly visible in profile rows; tag-page placement (e.g. #030 under an Indian hip-hop tag) is not equivalent to an official track genre. No full controlled vocabulary is evidenced.
- **Titles:** [PUBLIC—DIRECT] A common pattern is `NDCR #NNN: Artist`, but punctuation/spacing and capitalization vary. Some account tracks lack the number prefix. Treat public title as editorially authored; do not auto-generate a canonical title until NDCR confirms a standard.
- **Descriptions:** [PUBLIC—DIRECT] At least two indexed entries have substantial promotional bio-like copy; one excerpt has only account/profile links; profile rows expose titles/timestamps without description. Description presence and style are inconsistent in available evidence. The example text appears promotional but cannot be generalized to all sets.
- **Platform metadata:** SoundCloud exposes an account, title, timestamp, artwork image indicator, and on some individual indexed tracks description, genre, and links. YouTube could not be inspected; per-set YouTube pairing, duration, captions, thumbnail availability, and channel title conventions are UNKNOWN. SoundCloud audio duration was not reliably exposed in the collected results.
- **Instagram-only information:** UNKNOWN due inaccessible feed. Schedule artwork locally shows names/date/time, but does not show whether social posts contain biographies, event announcements, credits, behind-the-scenes material, or release corrections.
- **Exceptions:** The visible evidence includes unnumbered uploads and SoundCloud timestamps that differ from Thursday 18:00; these are exceptions to the proposed simplified patterns unless a distinction between planned time and platform upload time explains them. Incomplete access prevents firm historical claims.
- **Events/collaborations in set archive:** `HouseCats` and `Works Of Intent` may signal collaborator/partner-related material, but the profile title alone does not establish that. No set-to-event relationship was directly verified in returned official track metadata. Do not create relationships by name similarity alone.

## 6. Schema validation against `docs/ndcr-content-schema.md`

This section recommends changes for a later schema review. It does not modify the schema.

### Definitely needed or strongly justified

- A stable Set identity and a manually confirmed title: the account has numbered and unnumbered titles, and title punctuation/case vary.
- Set-to-Artist relationship(s): artist names occur in Set titles. Support multiple credited artists because local October art shows `Nac n Cheeze & Didar` on one slot, but validate that this indeed represents a joint set before modeling that specific record.
- Canonical external platform URL fields: SoundCloud URLs are evidenced. YouTube URLs are appropriate if cross-platform publishing is confirmed by the owner, but not verified by this platform audit.
- A platform publication timestamp/source field or a way to preserve external publication date independently from planned release time: SoundCloud’s visible publish timestamps differ from the local 18:00 schedule references. Do not conflate scheduled time with actual platform publication time.
- Optional description and optional genre: both appear on some SoundCloud entries; they are not consistently present. Preserve source text/genre attribution or provenance to avoid treating platform metadata as an NDCR-wide controlled taxonomy.
- Artwork/media relationship: image artwork is attached to at least some SoundCloud track pages and local references demonstrate variants. Rights, credit, provenance, and accessibility fields are necessary before using those assets on the site.
- Explicit content type or classification for account items that are not clearly numbered Sets. Do not force all SoundCloud tracks into the Set entity solely because they appear on the account.

### Optional / not currently demonstrated as universally needed

- `duration_seconds`: not reliably exposed in audited evidence; useful if collected, but not a required content-entry field until source availability and UX purpose are established.
- `event_ids` and `collaboration_ids` on Set: structurally useful only after explicit verified relationships exist. No such relation was confirmed in accessible set metadata.
- Detailed Artist biography, location, pronouns, portrait, artist genre description, photography, and public page status: only sparse profile information is visible, and most is not verified as artist-approved. Keep optional/consent-based.
- MonthlySchedulePublication record: local graphics support a recurring social schedule artifact, but a website schedule can be derived from confirmed Set dates. Record Instagram post URLs/status only if operations need them.
- Standalone Collaboration entity: deck mentions partners, but public platform evidence and partner-approved relationship details are not verified. A separate entity can wait until NDCR confirms it represents continuing work rather than event-specific co-promotion.

### Missing fields or distinctions worth considering after schema review

- `source_platform` plus `source_published_at` (or equivalent source-specific publication metadata) to distinguish publication on SoundCloud from the intended release slot and from publication elsewhere.
- Provenance/verification per imported field (e.g. platform page, approved schedule, editorial confirmation) and a value state such as unverified/confirmed. The audit surfaced mixed evidence strength; the schema currently has workflow state but no per-field/source provenance model.
- Preserve raw source title and normalized display title separately only if editorial/import workflows need it. Given observed punctuation/casing differences, at minimum avoid rewriting the external title silently.
- Classification for non-set uploads and potentially video-only or event/collaboration content, if those are confirmed. Avoid overloading Set or Collaboration prematurely.
- For media, source platform URL/thumbnail extraction provenance and permission-to-embed/use are distinct concerns. Existing `rights_status` is directionally right, but media origin/credit/licence evidence should be captured explicitly where available.

### Fields to derive

- Artist’s Set list from Set relationships; archive sorting from confirmed publication time; month grouping from the chosen canonical date; archive filter values from entered Set metadata.
- SoundCloud’s displayed genre should not automatically populate artist genres or a global canonical genre list.
- `format` must not be derived from SoundCloud presence, aspect ratio, a numbered set, or assumed alternation. Set format requires direct video/audio source verification or explicit editorial confirmation.
- Weekday, 18:00 IST, and weekly cadence are not supported as actual release timestamps by the observed SoundCloud dates. Keep them as a planning convention only if NDCR confirms current workflow; do not derive historical release dates/times from them.
- Set number is already part of public title/artwork for some records, but must be captured/verified, not calculated from count or chronology. Sequence gaps do not establish missing records.
- Event status can be derived from end date only if NDCR’s policy says so; cancellation and whether an event is still upcoming require editorial verification.

### Fields that should remain editorial/manual

- Public Set title/display casing; artist credit and multi-artist roles; description approval; genre labels; whether an upload is a Set, event/collaboration item, or another content type; actual release date/time if platform time is not the intended public release time; any format exception; featured homepage selections; archive visibility.
- Artist external links and public bio/image consent; event/collaboration facts and status; media credits, rights, relationships, and alt text; public use of schedule/event copy.
- The `featured_broadcast_label=live` state must be a human-confirmed live condition. No actual continuous/current live stream was verified.

### Unsupported assumptions in the current schema

- **Both YouTube and SoundCloud URLs are mandatory for every published Set.** SoundCloud is evidenced; YouTube’s per-release pairing and an every-release dual-publish rule were not verified.
- **Audio/video alternate `[video, audio]` on a confirmed cycle.** Not validated against the accessible archive or platform pairing.
- **Thursday at 18:00 IST is the actual release time.** Local schedule art shows it as planned schedule time; SoundCloud timestamps differ substantially. Planned vs actual needs separate handling.
- **One numbered Set per weekly slot / every account upload is a Set.** The profile contains unnumbered tracks; exact content type is unknown.
- **All public Set records have one or more Artists and a Set title.** This is likely for numbered artist sessions but should allow staging of ambiguous imports and non-set account items outside the Set entity.
- **A controlled global genre vocabulary exists or should be mandatory.** Only a few genre labels are visible and not consistent in completeness.
- **A complete 80+ archive exists in the current same numbering series.** This number appears in local deck/schema background, but this audit did not enumerate 80 records or confirm the count.
- **Every Set has both platform artwork variants or a web-cleared primary artwork.** References prove examples of variants, not completeness, publishing use, or rights status.
- **Set/event/collaboration relations are established from the deck or artwork.** They require explicit confirmation.

## WHAT WE NOW KNOW

- NDCR’s official SoundCloud account is named New Delhi Community Radio / NDCR and its accessible profile identifies New Delhi.
- At least eleven numbered track titles from #009 through #044 are identifiable in the accessible SoundCloud material; that set of observations is incomplete and does not imply every intermediate number exists or is missing.
- The SoundCloud titles commonly pair an NDCR number with an artist name, but punctuation/capitalization varies; at least two account uploads shown in the profile are unnumbered.
- SoundCloud exposes different combinations of date, artwork, genre, descriptions, and artist links; metadata completeness is uneven.
- Local references contain planned monthly schedule examples at 18:00 IST, an artwork example carrying `ADAMIX #070`, multiple platform-shaped artwork references, event/campaign art, and a community/photo collage. These remain local supporting evidence, not proof of release/publishing status or rights.
- The schema must separate planned schedule from platform publication timestamp, keep fields optional where evidence is inconsistent, and represent provenance/verification carefully.

## WHAT WE STILL DON'T KNOW

- Complete inventory of NDCR sets/releases and canonical numbering rules, including gaps, renumberings, unpublished slots, withdrawn releases, and unnumbered uploads.
- Whether YouTube has a paired video/audio release for each SoundCloud entry; actual format, duration, title, description, thumbnail, captions, and publication timestamp for each item.
- Whether Thursday 18:00 IST is a current schedule convention, an announcement time, or historically followed publication time; how postponements and missed weeks affect numbering and format.
- Full official Instagram feed, Story/highlight content, artist/event announcements, credits, and corrections.
- Which SoundCloud descriptions/artist links are approved and current, and the canonical artist names and profiles for the full archive.
- Actual event record/history, event status, venues, lineups, collaborators, ticket/RSVP links, access information, and documentation relationships.
- Full Drive asset inventory and rights/consent/credit records; whether local reference images can be used on the website.
- Whether collaborations should be maintained as standalone editorial records or attached only to particular events/sets.
- Whether the “80+” count and DEDHSAAL event/partner claims are accurate/current and approved for public site copy.

## WHAT SHOULD BE AUTOMATED

- After source facts are confirmed, derive archive and artist set listings from canonical relationships; sort by confirmed publication date; derive month labels and filter results from structured metadata.
- Validate URL format, required fields for the chosen publishing state, and duplicate external URLs/set numbers; keep validation warnings separate from source claims.
- Derive technical image properties and create responsive web derivatives only after rights/editorial approval.
- Offer schedule-slot suggestions only after the weekday/time/cadence and exception policy are reconfirmed. Keep the scheduled slot distinct from per-platform publication timestamps.
- Do not auto-assign genre, format, set number, artist profile, collaboration/event link, rights, or public live status from pattern matching.

## WHAT SHOULD REMAIN MANUAL

- Confirming what a platform upload represents, canonical artist/title/number, actual format, intended release date/time, description, genre, artist links, event/collaboration association, and release exceptions.
- Approving artist pages and bios, selecting featured/homepage content, and deciding public archive visibility.
- Confirming artwork identity, context, crop/focal point, credit, consent and rights status, alt text, event status, and which external links remain valid.
- Reconciliation between Instagram, YouTube, SoundCloud, schedule graphics, and the internal Set record until a trustworthy inventory is established.

## RECOMMENDED NEXT STEP

Obtain an owner-approved export or source-of-truth inventory of the full NDCR release catalogue, ideally including the official YouTube channel/video URLs, SoundCloud track URLs, Instagram schedule/post references, and the existing set-number/date/format record. Reconcile one Set at a time across platforms and mark each field’s source and confidence. In parallel, receive or inventory the Google Drive assets with creator/rights/consent details. Then review the schema against that verified inventory and decide which planning rules (Thursday/18:00, alternation, dual-platform publishing) are still valid. Do not begin CMS or page implementation until that reconciliation establishes a trustworthy V1 content set.
